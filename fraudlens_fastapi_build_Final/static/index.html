What FraudLens does

FraudLens reads a job posting the way a skeptical recruiter would — weighing company profile completeness alongside the wording itself — and returns two separate scores instead of one opaque verdict: a Content Risk score from a trained classifier, and a Verification Status from live company checks. Every flag comes with a plain-English reason, not just a label.
