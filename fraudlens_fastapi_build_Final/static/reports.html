How your report is used

Reporting a posting from the Analyzer stores it locally to help flag similar scams in future scans — it is not sent to any government body or company, and no individual report is shown publicly on this page. To actually report a scam, use the official channels:

  • NCRP (India) — cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930
  • BBB Scam Tracker — bbb.org/scamtracker
  • LinkedIn / Indeed / Naukri — use their "Report Job" feature directly

Periodically, reported postings are fed through the same candidate-phrase extraction used for the BBB pipeline: a phrase is only promoted into the live scam-keyword list once it's independently confirmed across 5 or more separate reports, filtered against a safe-list of common legitimate hiring words. This keeps the keyword layer current without any single report being able to skew it.
